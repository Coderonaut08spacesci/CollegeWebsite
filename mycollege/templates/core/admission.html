Admission Process

Follow these simple steps to enroll in your desired academic program.

  1. Check eligibility criteria for the chosen course.
  2. Fill out the online application form.
  3. Upload required documents (10th/12th Marksheet, Leaving Certificate).
  4. Pay the initial prospectus and processing fee online or at campus.

Required Documents

  • Class 10th and 12th original & photocopied marksheets
  • School / College Leaving Certificate
  • Passport size photographs (3 copies)
  • Category Certificate (if applicable)
